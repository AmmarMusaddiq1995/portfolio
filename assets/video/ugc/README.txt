Drop your UGC ad videos in this folder with these exact file names:
  - ugc-ad-1.mp4  -> Product Showcase Ad
  - ugc-ad-2.mp4  -> Testimonial-Style Ad
  - ugc-ad-3.mp4  -> Unboxing & Review Ad

Tips:
  - Vertical 9:16 videos (TikTok/Reels format) fit the cards perfectly.
  - Keep files under ~10-20 MB each so the page loads fast (compress with
    HandBrake or an online compressor if needed).
  - MP4 (H.264) is the safest format for all browsers.
  - Update each card's title and tags in index.html to match your actual ads.
  - Want more than 3 videos? Copy one of the <div class="col-lg-3 ..."> blocks
    inside the UGC Ads section in index.html.
